# Replace or skip a review participant

Ticket15 passed builder Windows/local-fake/browser checks and independent Sonnet review. Publication follows post-merge verification.

When a review turn needs attention, open **Replace or skip participant** in its recovery controls. Earlier accepted results stay in the run.

## Replace the participant

1. Choose a currently owned profile. The preview shows its provider, model and reasoning setting.
2. Review the role change and confirm replacing the current and future assignments for that role.
3. Select **Replace participant**.

The app creates a fresh instance from the selected profile's frozen settings. It keeps the selected profile-source seat running and supplies the replacement with the approved source, rules and accepted context. The original cast snapshot and completed contributions remain in the audit history.

Preparation can take a few seconds while the new worker proves its readiness. **Check replacement** shows its saved state. After a server restart, **Resume saved replacement** explicitly resumes the same decision and reserved turn identifiers.

If preparation fails before a brief is issued, **Repair failed preparation** targets that exact failed clone. A running failed clone requires explicit consent to end its local context. You can also choose a different profile with a fresh replacement decision. An issued brief permanently binds its owner; delivery failures use the current turn's normal saved-result or Retry controls.

## Skip the current contribution

Enter a reason, confirm the missing-work statement, then select **Skip contribution**. The app advances past this contribution and retains the role's later assignments.

The transcript records a missing-contribution notice. Later review briefs list the missing work separately from accepted answers. The synthesis and exported transcript retain the missing-work notice. Skipping the terminal output turn produces an explicit missing-output notice instead of a fabricated answer.

## Worker lifetime and repeated requests

Replacing or skipping content fences the old attempt. Its authenticated late output is rejected and quarantined. Its physical seat remains held until genuine worker and child/model exit evidence is available; other seat identities can continue. If that same seat is assigned a later turn, the turn waits for exit evidence or explicit participant replacement.

A repeated saved decision keeps its original identifiers and cannot advance twice. Stop permanently fences pending recovery. A corrected run has its own source and recovery scope.

Managed CLI replacement requires a verified fresh-context argument contract and available isolated settings. API replacement uses isolated request packets. Local worker exit does not prove remote provider computation has stopped. Native Linux/Omarchy and live AI-client compatibility remain outside this ticket's local Windows fake-client verification.
